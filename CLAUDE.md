## Project tracking (J-5 repo contract)

- Manifest: docs/PROJECT.md
- Todo list: docs/TODO.md
- Work log: docs/log.md

After finishing a piece of work in this repo: tick/add items in docs/TODO.md (move finished items to `## Done` with `(done YYYY-MM-DD @<device>)`), append a `docs/log.md` entry, commit with the code. Before starting work: read docs/TODO.md `## Done` and the last 10 log entries to avoid redoing finished work.
